SETUP
1. Upload index.html to the site root.
2. Upload css/main.css to css/main.css.
3. Upload js/app.js to js/app.js.
4. Do NOT delete or replace js/firebase.js.
5. Publish firestore.rules in Firebase Console.
6. Open the HTTPS website, log in, tap Current Location or Set on Map once, then switch Go Online.
